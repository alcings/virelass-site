Pricing

Pay for the homes you run

One price per occupied property per month. Empty plots and unsold units cost nothing. Every estate starts with a free 30-day trial.

Standard

For most estates.

Per home

  • Residents' app, guard app and web app
  • Visitor passes, events, walk-ins and contractor permits
  • Dues, reminders and online payments
  • Panic alerts, incidents and emergency mode
  • An SMS and WhatsApp allowance each month

Premium

For large estates and busy gates.

Per home

  • Everything in Standard
  • A larger SMS and WhatsApp allowance
  • Gate controllers, tags and number-plate cameras across several gates
  • Priority support

Good to know

  • Pay yearly and get months free
  • Messages beyond the allowance are billed on the next invoice
  • Residents pay dues straight to the estate's account; Virelass never holds that money
  • If an invoice is missed, gates, guards and residents keep working
